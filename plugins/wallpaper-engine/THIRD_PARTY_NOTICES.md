# Third-party notices

The Wallpaper Engine plugin is maintained by Matsuki and declares the MIT license
in `plugin.json`. This migration preserves the bundled libraries and their
original attribution.

- **WebWallGL 3.x** — bundled in `lib/webwallgl/engine/webwallgl.global.min.js`.
  The supplied source documentation identifies it as MIT-licensed. The adapter
  files remain in `lib/webwallgl/`.
- **jpeg-js 0.4.4** — bundled in `lib/vendor-jpeg-js/`. Its upstream package
  metadata, copyright notices, and full license text are retained in that
  directory, including `LICENSE` and the decoder/encoder source headers.

Wallpaper Engine projects and workshop media are not included in this plugin.
